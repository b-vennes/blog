# About Me

I'm a software engineer currently between jobs with a ton of experience working
on lots of different types of projects. I most recently worked at The Walt
Disney Company on the Streaming Data Platform team building an awesome schema
registry for records in data streams. Before that, I worked at a little startup
on blockchain infrastructure. And before that, I was a full-stack software
engineer building construction management applications.

I'm always experimenting with new tools and ways of doing things. Most recently
my expertise has been in Scala, but I haven't let that stop me from deep diving
into the complexities of the web development stack.

When I'm not programming, I'm usually hanging out at the
[Austin Pinball Collective](https://austinpinballcollective.org/), watching
Formula One, or seeing the newest films with my wife Kenzi.
